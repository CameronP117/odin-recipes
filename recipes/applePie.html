Apple Pie Recipe

Home Page

This classic Apple Pie relies on crisp, tart apples and a flaky pastry crust to create a timeless, comforting dessert. The filling combines thinly sliced Granny Smith apples with fresh lemon juice, granulated sugar, light brown sugar, all-purpose flour, ground cinnamon, nutmeg, and a pinch of salt. Everything is baked inside a golden-brown double pie crust, which is brushed with a simple egg wash and a sprinkle of coarse sugar for a beautifully crisp finish.
